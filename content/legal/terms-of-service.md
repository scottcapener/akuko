## 6. AI-generated writing is not allowed

Writing it yourself is supposed to be the fun part, and the purpose of Hot Cocoa is to help it feel that way.

You may not use Hot Cocoa to store, organize, or develop manuscript text that was generated by a large language model or similar system and kept as your own work. That includes generating scenes, chapters, dialogue, or description with such a model, and expanding your outline into prose with one.

Ordinary writing tools are entirely fine: spelling and grammar checkers, dictionaries, thesauruses, translation tools, and research you did with help from anything at all and then wrote up in your own words.

**How we enforce this.** We do not run AI-detection software on your manuscripts. In addition to our commitment to never letting AI ingest your work as training data, AI detection tools are often unreliable, frequently producing false accusations against real writers. This happens most often against people writing in a second language or in an unusual voice, and we are not willing to act on a machine's guess about your prose. We act on human judgment, and only when something is actually brought to our attention.

If we believe this rule has been broken, we will tell you what we think happened and give you a chance to respond before we act, except where Section 8 allows us to move immediately.
